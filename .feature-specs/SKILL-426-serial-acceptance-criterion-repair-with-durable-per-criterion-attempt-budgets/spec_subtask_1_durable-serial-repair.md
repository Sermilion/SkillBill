## Acceptance Criteria

1. S1-001. The durable model and admission code retain every admitted authored criterion's canonical reference, original alias and unmodified text, spec ordinal/digest, catalog and execution identity. Initial structured audit admission demands exactly one explicit source disposition and validation-obligation record per criterion, rejects omissions/duplicates/unknown identities, and retains composite criteria as one identity and budget. Covers AC-001 and AC-006.
2. S1-002. Dependency admission records dependent/prerequisite identities, reason and declaring-output/source evidence provenance. It rejects unknown references, self-edges, cycles and unavailable validation-owned prerequisites that make source repair impossible. The unresolved-production queue uses stable topological order with admitted ordinal ties; already source-satisfied prerequisites remain evidence facts. Covers AC-001.
3. S1-003. Pure serial transition operations represent initial discovery, current-criterion-targeted and final-full modes, one current AC, one live reservation and immutable outcome identities. They preserve unsuccessful totals through success, reopening and restart, cap each authored AC at three unsuccessful outcomes, record final-audit-required for an empty queue and allow only final-full admission to reopen demonstrated regressions. Counts use explicit admitted source/validation facts and allow mixed criteria to be source-closed and validation-deferred. Covers AC-003, AC-005 and AC-006 foundations.
4. S1-004. Engine-owned persistence operations use the existing workflow artifact storage and required persistence boundary. Their same acquired-owner-fenced transaction checks active lease/token/generation and expected revision, catalog/execution identity, current AC and reservation, and couples accepted serial changes with relevant phase, ledger, attempt evidence and workflow changes. Detached claims/readers cannot mutate state. In-memory acceptance and success publication occur only after commit. Covers AC-004.
5. S1-005. Outcome persistence accepts an identical accepted outcome replay once, refuses conflicting/stale output without mutation, preserves authoritative counters/evidence across reopen and restart, and treats post-commit filesystem projection failure as committed-with-projection-failure with projection-only regeneration. Failed commit publishes no closure or successful report. Critical identity, totals and closure/reopen provenance survive bounded display-history pruning. Covers AC-004.
6. S1-006. Repair state, structured audit result, repair handoff and adoption artifacts have strict Draft 2020-12 YAML schemas starting at 0.1, owning versions/keys/enums, named durable families, dispatch/packaged resources, parity and rejection tests, coherence checks and typed failure codes at every parse seam. Absent unsafe legacy state and malformed/present-wrong-version state cannot become an empty queue or zero budget. Covers AC-008.
7. S1-007. Legacy adoption code explicitly enumerates supported stopped-pristine archived standalone, goal-child-build and goal-child-validate audit compositions at semantic revisions 1, 2 and 3, checks their exact historical descriptor/policy/skeleton/version and evidence tuple, and records separate validated provenance. Prior repair, in-flight/ambiguous history, identity/digest conflicts or an unstopped owner refuse adoption. Original descriptors, outputs, aggregate retries, ledger and checkpoints remain preserved; fresh counts never claim reconstructed historical attempts. Strategy revision 4 is not activated by this subtask. Covers AC-008.
8. S1-008. Authored domain/validator and isolated production SQLite regressions assert unsafe/safe admission, stable ordering/composite identity, once-only reservation/outcome behavior, restart/replay/fencing, coupled rollback, failed-commit publication and projection-only recovery through observable results. Each new test states its realistic bug; real past regressions and governed parity/guard coverage remain intact. Required persistence propagates cancellation and emits bounded attributable refusal/recovery/degradation diagnostics without weakening guards or wire ownership. Covers AC-009 and AC-010.
