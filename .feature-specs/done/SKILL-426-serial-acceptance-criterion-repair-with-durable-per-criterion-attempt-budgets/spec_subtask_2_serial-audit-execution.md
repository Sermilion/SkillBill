## Acceptance Criteria

1. S2-001. Initial, focused and final audits use existing phase envelopes with prose findings and normal verdicts. Plans and repair reports receive readable current-criterion context through existing briefing channels. No exact catalog/disposition response, required evidence field, phrase/tag format or dependency graph gates progress. Remove the old feature's audit-result, repair-handoff, repair-state and adoption schemas, validators and dedicated registrations, and code used only by that contract pipeline. Covers AC-001, AC-002 and AC-008.
2. S2-002. The running repair path reserves its current cycle before mutation and settles one outcome through the acquired-owner transaction with phase records, ledger, attempts and workflow advancement. An incomplete or retryable blocked repair charges once; otherwise failed targeted audit charges that same cycle once. Memory and successful reports change only after commit. Covers AC-003 and AC-004.
3. S2-003. Focused planning and repair stay on the runtime-selected authored criterion until source acceptance or its third failure. Discovery, read-only clarification and planning consume no allowance. Generic semantic retry loops do not grant extra criterion repairs. Successful shared edits cannot close or skip another criterion. Covers AC-001, AC-002 and AC-003.
4. S2-004. Every path into review or resumed source completion requires final full prose audit of the current source and authored production requirements. A demonstrated regression reopens its original criterion with retained failures. An exhausted reopened criterion blocks immediately. Empty queue or repair completion alone is insufficient. Covers AC-005.
5. S2-005. The audit explains source gaps separately from later test/build obligations, including mixed criteria. Source acceptance does not fabricate quality evidence or full completion. Reports use known facts rather than total-minus-prose-gaps arithmetic. No new obligation receipt or disposition schema is required. Covers AC-006.
6. S2-006. Startup, restart and duplicate delivery preserve committed current focus, live cycle and retained budgets. Stale ownership and failed commit cannot publish acceptance. Projection recovery does not replay outcomes. Existing historical prose and aggregate attempts remain unchanged, with unavailable attribution explained rather than invented. Covers AC-004 and AC-008.
7. S2-007. Canonical and delegating audit strategies implement the same prose loop through existing owners and command surface. Budget blocks carry the current criterion, retained count and latest readable gap in existing report text. Remove schema-driven adoption gates, fixed historical tuple reconstruction and structured-evidence-only permission checks. Preserve unrelated execution-plan compatibility, process protections and existing outer contracts. Covers AC-007, AC-008 and AC-010.
8. S2-008. A few runner/persistence regressions demonstrate focused routing, once-only failure accounting, third-failure blocking, final-audit gating, retained-budget regression and rollback/restart behavior. Ordinary prose findings work without schema fields or required markers. Tests assert transitions and visible results, not exact prose or discarded schema contracts. Covers AC-009 and AC-010.
