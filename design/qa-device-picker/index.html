What picking does. The selection is the device QA runs started from the app use. "Let QA choose" keeps today's behavior: the pack picks a free device or boots an AVD. Picking a stopped AVD does not boot it here. QA boots it when the run starts, which the pack already supports for a named AVD. Unauthorized and offline devices can't be picked and show the fix. A device another run holds a lease on can't be picked either, since the pack would settle skipped. The list refreshes when the dropdown opens and on the refresh button. Nothing polls in the background.
